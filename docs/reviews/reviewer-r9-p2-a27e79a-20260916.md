## Challenged invariant

Authenticated canonical Carrier ACK feedback has three materially different outcomes and process/result truth must preserve them:

1. **applied** — the ACK actually retires at least one Recovery packet;
2. **accepted-empty** — a canonical stale/duplicate ACK is accepted by Recovery but retires no packet;
3. **rejected** — Recovery rejects the ACK, including a future/never-sent range.

Accepted-empty feedback is neither a packet transition nor a rejection. A rejected future/never-sent ACK must remain fail-closed and must not mutate RTT/PTO/loss/cwnd/packet or Session state.

This review does not alter ACK framing, Session semantics, Carrier architecture, crypto/wire architecture, malformed budgets, deadlines, or policy values.
