## How the target was found

A whole-repository zero-call-site sweep: every `pub fn` in `crates/*/src/*.rs` was
extracted and each name counted across the whole tree in all call forms
(`.name(`, `::name(`, bare `name(`), then discounted for associated functions,
field reads and free functions. Exactly one genuine never-called public method
remained: **`VersionNegotiator::is_established`** (zero call sites anywhere,
including neko-cli, the only consumer of the type). Both its polarity and its
match arm were therefore free.

The new test pins it by requiring the three accessors to agree across every
negotiation state - Awaiting; Established(0) on both the client and the server
side of a real handshake; and Rejected via each of the two rejection paths
(undecodable hello, and no-compatible-version). A standalone literal assertion
would let the trio drift apart, so agreement is what is pinned.

Its neighbours `state()`, `selected()` and `NegotiationBinding::as_bytes()` were
probed at the same time and are already covered: mutants returning a constant, the
wrong state, `Some(v + 1)` or an empty slice are all killed by the existing
061750a tests.
